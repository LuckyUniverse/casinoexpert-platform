**Methodology caveat for AskGamblers** (important — include this verbatim in your notes for every brand):

> AskGamblers' complaint resolution rate is calculated based on operators that opt into AskGamblers' dispute process. AskGamblers has refused to register as a formally recognized Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) body — unlike eCOGRA — so most regulated operators (including Super Partners and Casino Rewards brands) do not route disputes through them. A low AskGamblers CRM score does NOT mean complaints go unresolved at these brands; it means disputes are handled through formal ADRs (eCOGRA, MGA-recognized bodies) instead. Capture the AskGamblers score for completeness but do not treat it as a primary signal.

**Real trust signals to elevate** (these matter more than AskGamblers):

- **eCOGRA seal** in the operator footer — a recognized ADR. Note if displayed and any audit dates visible.
- **MGA license** — cross-check the license number (visible in footer) against the [MGA portal](https://www.mga.org.mt/support/online-gaming-licence-search/) if possible.
- **Game fairness audits** — iTech Labs, GLI, NMi seals.

## Tone & voice

These are research notes, but the eventual published pages will lean positive-promotional (we're recommending these brands to readers). Your notes should support that voice — capture mixed feedback fairly but don't weaponize negatives.

**Language to avoid in your notes** (and that absolutely will not appear on the eventual pages):

- "complaint," "complaints"
- "cons," "negatives," "drawbacks," "downsides"
- Heading patterns like "Pros & cons," "Strengths & weaknesses," "What we like / don't like"

**Language to prefer:**

- "what players notice," "worth knowing," "where it really shines"
- "the trade-off is…," "fair warning…," "real-world experience"
- "consistent themes from players," "areas where the brand could lean further in"

**Examples of how to phrase mixed feedback:**

- ❌ *"Complaints about slow withdrawals."*
- ✅ *"First-time withdrawals sit a couple of days while verification clears — repeat withdrawals via Interac typically arrive same-day."*

- ❌ *"Cons: occasional support delays."*
- ✅ *"Live chat is the fastest support channel; email replies can take 24 hours during peak periods."*

Generally: be factual, be specific (numbers + sources beat generalities), and frame honestly without leaning negative.
